Questions

Which weight should I get?

Pro .045 is good for most jobs and is easier to carry. Heavy Duty .060 is thicker and heavier, so it grips harder and stands up to more abuse. Pros who are on a job every day usually go Heavy Duty.

What is it made of?

EPDM, the commercial rubber membrane used on flat roofs. It's made to sit outside in sun and weather for decades.

How do I clean it?

Wipe it down or hose it off, let it dry, and roll it up for the next job.

Can I pick it up, or do you ship?

Both. Pick up in York, PA, or call (717) 577-5300 for shipping and we'll quote the freight to your door.

Can you put my company name on it?

Yes. We print your business name, logo or website down the runner in white or yellow. Every time a client walks your runner, they see who's taking care of their floors. Call for custom pricing.

Do you do bigger sizes?

Yes. Drops come off a 10' wide roll, so 10' x 15', 10' x 20' and longer can be cut to order.
